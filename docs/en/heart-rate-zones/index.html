R (repetition) efforts end within 2 minutes, before heart rate catches up. Go by pace, not heart rate.

Training by heart rate

  • On hot days, short-sleep days or after caffeine, heart rate runs higher at the same pace. On those days it's safer to follow heart rate than pace.
  • Wrist sensors lag behind in sessions where heart rate rises and falls quickly, like intervals. They're reliable for easy and long runs.
  • If your easy-run heart rate keeps going above the E range, slow down. Walk breaks are fine.
