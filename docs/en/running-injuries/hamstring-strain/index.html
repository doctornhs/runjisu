Hamstring strain

Where it hurts · Back of the thigh · Also called Back-of-thigh muscle injury

A muscle injury felt as a sudden twinge in the back of the thigh while running fast. In distance runners it also often shows up not as a sudden injury but as a tendon problem, a gradual ache just below the buttock.

If it feels like this

  • A sudden twinge or pull in the back of the thigh during strides or intervals.
  • The back of the thigh pulls and hurts when you reach the leg forward or bend at the waist.
  • A deep ache below the buttock when sitting long or running fast may be a tendon problem.

Why it happens

  • More likely when you run fast without warming up.
  • Speed work while fatigued is a common trigger.
  • A hamstring injured once is easy to injure again.

What you can do yourself

  • On the day you feel a twinge, stop fast running immediately.
  • For the first few days walk within a pain-free range, and avoid hard stretching through pain.
  • Once the pain settles, start light strengthening such as bridges and build up the intensity.
  • Hold off on strides and intervals until easy running is pain-free.

See a doctor if

  • You felt a "pop" or have wide bruising on the back of your thigh
  • It's hard to walk or your leg feels weak
  • Pain below the buttock lasts more than a month

This guide is general information and does not replace a diagnosis. Pain in the same spot can have different causes, so see a doctor if symptoms are clear or last.
