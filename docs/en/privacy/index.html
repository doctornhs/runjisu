Privacy policy

Runjisu is a calculator you use without signing up. Here's what is stored and where.

What you enter

We don't collect names, contact details or account information. Results, age, sex, weight, heart rate, run logs and shoe details you enter are stored only in your browser on this device and are never sent to our servers.

Everything stored is erased if you clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Analytics, ads and affiliate links

Analytics We use Google Analytics to count visits and popular pages. In the process Google collects cookies and device information. Values you enter in the tools are not sent. You can opt out by blocking cookies or using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Weather and air quality The home-screen weather is fetched by sending your chosen area's coordinates to Open-Meteo. Your device location is used only if you tap "My location", and only coordinates rounded to about 10 km are sent. Your IP address reaches Open-Meteo's servers in the process. Your chosen area and the weather data are stored only on your device.

Affiliate links Buy buttons in the gear guide are Coupang Partners links. When you go to Coupang through one, Coupang uses cookies to see whether you buy, and if you do, we earn a commission. Your price doesn't change.

Third parties and contact

We don't collect personal information, so there is none to share with third parties.
