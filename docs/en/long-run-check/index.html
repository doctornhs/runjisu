A study that followed 5,205 runners for 18 months found injuries were more clearly linked to the distance of a single run than to weekly distance. Overuse injuries rose on days runners went more than 10% beyond their longest run of the last 30 days.

In the same study, weekly increases weren't clearly linked to injury. The popular 10% weekly rule is a rule of thumb; treat it as a reference only.

When pain matters more than distance

  • Pressing one spot on your shin, top of the foot or heel bone hurts
  • It worsens as you run, or hurts on rest days and at night
  • It's swollen, or you limp when walking

If any of these apply, stop running and see a doctor. Problems like stress fractures heal faster the earlier you rest.
