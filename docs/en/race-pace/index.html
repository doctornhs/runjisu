Using your splits

  • The first 2–3 km feel easy, so it's tempting to run faster than your splits. Time banked there gets paid back several times over later.
  • On climbs, keep your effort steady, not your pace. The splits assume a flat course.
  • Heat and humidity slow you down at the same effort. Lowering your goal is how you protect your finish time.
  • Marathon splits only hold if you've built enough long-run training to last beyond 30 km.
