Crew runs,
split by pace

Paste your members' times, one person per line, and runners of similar fitness are grouped together, each group with an easy pace everyone can hold. Names and times are stored only on this device.

Members' times

Example This is a sample list. Clear it and paste your crew's.

Write the distance as 5k, 10k, half or full, and the time like 55:00 or 1:58:30. In auto mode, runners whose Runjisu differs by more than 4 go into different groups.

Groups and easy pace

A group's pace is the easy range of its slowest runner. It is for conversational runs; do intervals and tempo runs at your own pace.
