Preparing without injury

  • Ramping up intervals to chase a 10K time loads your calves and Achilles. Keep intervals under 8% of weekly distance per session.
  • One or two fast sessions a week is enough; fill the rest with an easy, conversational pace.
  • If your heel or Achilles is stiff and sore on your first steps in the morning for several days, cut fast sessions first.

Frequently asked questions

What pace per km is 10km in 55 minutes?

5:30 per km. Run it evenly and you'll finish in 55:00. On a 400 m track, that's 2:12 per lap.

What speed should I set on a treadmill?

11.0 km/h or faster. With no wind resistance, a treadmill can feel a little easier than outdoors at the same speed.

If I can run 10km in 55 minutes, what can I expect in the half and full marathon?

At the same aerobic level: 5K about 26:29, Half about 2:01:56, Full about 4:11:36. These assume proper preparation for each distance; without enough long runs, expect slower times.
