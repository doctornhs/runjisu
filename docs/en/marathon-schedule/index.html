Once you've picked a race

  • Marathon training usually starts 16–20 weeks out; half marathon training 10–12 weeks out.
  • Some races, such as the Seoul Marathon full course, require a qualifying finish time to enter. Set a certified time the year before your goal race.
  • If you miss the entry lottery, find another race in the same season. Your training plan still works.
