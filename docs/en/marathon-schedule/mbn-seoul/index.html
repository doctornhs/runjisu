Injury notes from an orthopedic surgeon

  • For a half marathon, steadily building your weekend long run is what holds up the second half. It's safest not to go more than 10% beyond your longest run of the last 30 days.
  • Fill most of your training with an easy, conversational pace, and keep threshold work under 10% of weekly distance per session.
  • If one specific spot hurts while running and worsens as you go, stop for the day.
