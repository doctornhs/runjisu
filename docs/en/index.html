How to read the results

  • Runjisu shares its units with maximal oxygen uptake (VO2max) but also reflects running economy. Recreational runners often score lower than lab measurements, and lower still when it's calculated from half or full marathon results.
  • Predicted times assume equal preparation for that distance. A marathon prediction from a 10K result alone comes out faster than reality if your long runs fall short.
  • Training paces reflect your current fitness. Enter a recent result, not your goal time.
